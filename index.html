Understand Margin and Leverage Before Trading

Margin allows a user to open a position that is larger than the available account balance. This can increase possible returns, but it can also increase losses very quickly. Before using leverage, ask:

  • What is the margin level?
  • Does margin change by market?
  • Are overnight positions allowed?
  • When will a position close automatically?
  • Can losses exceed the account balance?
  • Are extra charges applied?
  • How quickly can margin requirements change?

High leverage should never be presented as free money. A small price move can cause a large loss when the position size is too high.
